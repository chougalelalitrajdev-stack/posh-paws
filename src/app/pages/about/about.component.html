Our story

Six years of caring
for Pune's pets

Posh Paws was born from a simple belief — that every pet deserves the same quality of care their owner gives themselves. We started in a small studio in Koregaon Park and have grown into Pune's most trusted grooming sanctuary, one happy pet at a time.

Our groomers are certified professionals who genuinely love animals. We use only imported, pet-safe, sulphate-free products — nothing harsh, nothing that doesn't belong near your beloved companion.
